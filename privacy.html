Introduction

TV96 Live provides football information and editorial content, including fixtures, scores, standings, news, match previews, and access to match-related media where available. This policy explains what information may be handled when you visit TV96 Live, why it is used, and which third parties may receive technical data.

Information We Handle

Information you provide

TV96 Live does not currently provide user accounts, a checkout, or an on-site contact form. If you email support@tv96live.org, we and the email service involved may handle your email address, message, attachments, and related delivery metadata so that we can review and respond to your enquiry.

Information handled automatically

When you visit, our hosting, analytics, advertising, and content providers may receive technical and usage information. Depending on the service and your settings, this may include your IP address, browser and device type, operating system, language, referring page, pages viewed, timestamps, approximate location inferred from IP address, request details, performance data, and interactions such as clicks.

We use this information to deliver the website, protect and troubleshoot it, understand general usage, improve performance and content, display advertising, and respond to legitimate legal or security requirements.

Analytics and Hosting

The site is hosted on Vercel. Vercel may process request, network, device, location-derived, and performance information to deliver and protect the site. TV96 Live also uses Vercel Web Analytics on pages where its script is present to understand website usage and performance.

Some pages use Umami Analytics to measure visits and interactions. Information sent to Umami may include page URLs, referral information, browser and device details, event data, and technical request information, depending on the service configuration.

For more information, see the official Vercel Privacy Notice and Umami Privacy Policy.

Advertising

TV96 Live uses Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, local storage, device identifiers, IP addresses, and information about visits or interactions to deliver, measure, limit, and protect ads. Ads may be personalized where permitted and enabled, or non-personalized depending on consent, location, account settings, and Google's systems.

TV96 Live does not control all data collected or retained by Google and its advertising partners. You can learn more from Google's advertising privacy information, review the Google Privacy Policy, and manage personalization through Google Ads Settings.

Cookies, Local Storage, and Offline Caching

The website stores your light or dark theme preference in your browser's local storage. If you use the site's installable web-app or offline features, a service worker may place core site files in browser cache storage so an offline page and selected assets can remain available.

Third-party services, particularly advertising providers, may use cookies or similar browser storage for their own purposes. You can block or delete cookies and site data through your browser settings, although doing so may affect advertising, saved preferences, offline access, or other features.

Football Data and Media

Fixtures, scores, results, and standings may be obtained from football-data.org through TV96 Live's server-side API routes and combined with public football information maintained by the site. Requests made by your browser to those TV96 Live API routes may be included in ordinary hosting logs.

Team and competition images may load directly from media.api-sports.io. When an external image is requested, that provider may receive standard connection information such as your IP address, browser details, referring page, and request time.

Pages offering match-related media use HLS.js delivered through jsDelivr and may connect directly to external media delivery hosts. Those hosts can receive normal request and device information and may apply their own storage or logging practices. Availability and providers may change over time.

Third-Party Services

In addition to the services described above, pages may request libraries or styling from cdnjs, jsDelivr, and Tailwind's content delivery network. Social links may lead to Facebook, TikTok, or YouTube. These providers operate independently and may process information under their own terms and privacy policies when your browser contacts them or you follow a link.

Third parties determine many details of their own processing, security, and retention. TV96 Live does not control those independent practices.

External Links

TV96 Live may link to external websites, social networks, sources, or services. Following an external link takes you to a service that has its own privacy practices. Review that service's privacy information before providing personal information.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to maintain the website and limit unnecessary collection. However, no internet transmission, email system, browser storage, or third-party service can be guaranteed completely secure. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information by email unless it is necessary.

Data Retention

We keep email correspondence only for as long as reasonably needed to handle the enquiry, maintain relevant records, resolve disputes, protect the service, or meet legal obligations. Hosting, analytics, advertising, content, and email providers set retention periods for information they process under their own policies and configurations. Retention periods vary by provider and the purpose for which information is processed.

Children's Privacy

TV96 Live is a general-audience football website and is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us, contact us so we can review the matter and take appropriate action.

Your Choices and Rights

You can manage cookies, local storage, and cached site data in your browser; disable ad personalization in Google Ads Settings; and choose not to follow external links. Depending on where you live, applicable law may give you rights concerning personal information, such as requesting access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection.

To ask about information handled directly by TV96 Live, email support@tv96live.org. We may need enough information to understand and verify a request. For data controlled by a third-party provider, you may also need to contact that provider directly.

Policy Changes

We may update this policy when the website's features, providers, or legal obligations change. The date at the top identifies the latest published version. Material changes will be reflected on this page.
