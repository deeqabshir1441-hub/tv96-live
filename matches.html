Coverage and match times

TV96 displays provider-supplied Premier League and Champions League fixtures, plus selected clubs from La Liga (Real Madrid, Barcelona, Atlético Madrid), Serie A (Milan, Inter, Juventus, Napoli, Roma), Bundesliga (Bayern Munich, Dortmund) and Ligue 1 (Paris Saint-Germain). This is a selected fixture feed, not every match in those four domestic leagues.

Yesterday, Today and Tomorrow use East Africa Time (Africa/Nairobi, UTC+3). Kick-off times use that same zone. An ongoing match that crosses midnight stays in Today while live. The Updated label uses your browser's local time and records when the display was refreshed, not when the provider last confirmed a score.

Fixtures, scores and statuses come from football-data.org; occasional featured fixtures may be curated separately. Updates can be delayed or unavailable. An empty day means no matching fixtures were returned for our coverage, not that no football is being played. Try another day; if loading fails, reload later or consult the competition's official fixtures.
