Reading this table

GD

Pts

Position colours are indicative guides. European places can change with cup winners, title holders and season-specific allocations; relegation and play-off rules also vary by competition. A highlighted position is not confirmation of qualification or relegation. See the European qualification guide and tiebreaker explanation.

Tables depend on football-data.org and may lag completed matches. An empty or failed response cannot establish a club's position. Reload later, select another competition, or check the official league table before treating an outcome as final.
